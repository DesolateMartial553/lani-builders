How a job runs

From the first look at your home to the final walk-through.

  1. Site visit

    Lani walks the space with you, looks at your ideas and any drawings, and talks through access, rough measurements and what needs to stay usable during the work.

  2. Written quote

    The scope, materials and costs set out in writing, including what is excluded and any decisions still to be made.

  3. Agree the schedule

    The order of work, access arrangements and any approvals needed are confirmed before a start date is agreed.

  4. The build

    Work moves through the agreed stages. Any change to scope or cost is discussed before extra work goes ahead.

  5. Snagging

    A walk-through of the finished work together, with any outstanding items listed and a plan to put them right.

  6. Sign-off & handover

    Final check of the agreed work, relevant paperwork and care instructions handed over.

Common Questions

What clients usually want to know before starting a job.

What should I have ready for a quote?

Rough sizes, photos, your postcode and a preferred timescale are a useful start. Include drawings if you already have them, and mention any access restrictions.

How quickly can I get a quote?

Usually within 48 hours of an initial site visit or call.

Are you insured?

Yes, we're fully insured for all work we carry out, and we're happy to show you the paperwork.

Do I need planning permission for an extension?

It depends on the size and scope — we'll advise during your quote and can point you toward the right process.

Can I stay at home during the work?

Usually, yes. Tell us at the site visit which rooms you need to use, and we'll plan around dust, access and keeping the kitchen working where we can.

Do you clean up after the job?

Yes, we keep sites tidy throughout and leave the property clean on completion.
